What data the tool uses

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) for that single account. This is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and to show which account is connected.

Storage

Tokens and videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to third parties, except to TikTok when uploading a video.

Other users

The tool does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); locally stored tokens are then deleted.
